# MVP Scope

## Metadata
- Status: In Review
- Owner: Person 5 Research
- Last Updated: 2026-04-24


## Included User Flow
- Sleep/tap-to-wake kiosk entry.
- Language selection: English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam.
- Chat and voice interaction modes.
- Result/info display with concise receptionist guidance.

## Included Capabilities
- Admissions and fee information guidance.
- Department, office, and HOD lookup.
- Indoor navigation help using mapped floorplan data.
- Escalation to human staff when uncertain/high-risk.

## Excluded from MVP
- End-to-end transactional workflows (payments/forms).
- Broad omnichannel deployment beyond lobby kiosk.
- Advanced admin governance beyond admin-lite.
