# DEC-001 Product Direction

## Metadata
- Status: Approved
- Owner: Person 3 Fullstack
- Last Updated: 2026-04-24


## Decision
CLARA is a voice-first, touch-second lobby kiosk focused on receptionist tasks, not a general campus chatbot.

## Rationale
- Matches real lobby usage pattern and user expectations.
- Keeps MVP focused on highest-value intents.

## Implications
- UX and latency priorities favor quick, practical responses.
- Scope excludes non-lobby transactional features in pilot phase.
